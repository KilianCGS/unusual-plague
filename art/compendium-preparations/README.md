# Compendium preparation illustrations

New illustrations generated with ChatGPT image generation, not PixelLab.
Kilian approved all fifteen final images on 2026-11-08. No existing ingredient
page or inventory icon was replaced. Runtime copies live in
`public/game/ui/book/preparations/`. Preserve these approved images.

Style reference: `public/game/ui/book/ingredients/es/ingredients-01.png`.
Direction: warm brown pen-and-ink herbalist engraving, natural proportions,
crosshatching, isolated objects, plain white background, no lettering or book
frame. Names and recipes remain localized, live game text.

The initial transparent generation had destructive cutouts and was rejected.
The final images use white paper and multiply blending in the Book, preserving
the complete ink drawing while showing the existing parchment beneath it.

Each file is named after one of the fifteen existing recipe output ids.
Objects reflect those existing preparations; no new recipe or ingredient was
introduced for an illustration. All final images use the ingredient page as
their style reference.

Review in `/dev/book`: choose **Ver los 15 preparados**, open the cover and turn
pages. ES/EN switches are development-only, as is the full recipe fixture.
Illustrations are scaled with `contain` to preserve their proportions.
